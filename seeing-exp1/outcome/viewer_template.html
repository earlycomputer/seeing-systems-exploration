Seeing Systems · Experiment 1b

See the Outcome

Can a model correct a shot when it can only judge it by where the ball went? Opus 5.5 wrote the shot. The harness then missed it on purpose, each model was shown one readback of the miss, and MuJoCo judged every correction.

The shots

Opus's own shot, which goes in as written, and the three misses made from it with a one-line edit to its launch. Drawn as the experiment's drafting view: a side elevation over a plan from above, at one scale, in metres. A ball is drawn every 0.1 s, older copies lighter.

Every run

Each square is one run: one model, one readback, one miss, one seed. Pick a square to see what the model was shown, what it said, and where its corrections flew.
