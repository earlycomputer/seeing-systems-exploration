# 1h notes

- A ball resting on the floor can lose contact for a step while it settles, so "ball touches floor" can read as a
  touch that begins after the start. The brief writer is told never to test touches with the floor.
- Raw state at 10 rows a second came out about the same size as the run in words on the dry-run worlds (2,076 against
  1,474 chars). On bigger worlds raw grows with bodies × time; words grow with events. Worth watching: that is where a
  large ratio could show.
- Reading a language world costs its parts library (about 13 KB) on every read. That is a real cost of the format
  and is counted.
- 1g also found 37 expectation lines that needed forms the checker can't express ("rises at least 0.5 m"). 1h's
  hidden tests are limited to the same forms, so the brief writer may steer toward touches, rests and drops. The
  tests can only measure what those forms say.
