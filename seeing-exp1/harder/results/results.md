## Summary

1. 112 worlds, 0 failed runs, $16.89 (Opus 5.5 $13.58, GPT-6.1 Sol $3.30), under the $25 Jono approved. With experiments 1 to 1c, $65.65 of the $100 cap.
2. Broken on purpose, the picture fixed 28 of 40 worlds and text only 17 of 40. Opus 5.5: 12 against 9 of 20. GPT-6.1 Sol: 16 against 8 of 20.
3. The split is by where the failure lives. Where it can be worked out from the file (the shot's backspin, the cup's distance), text fixed 16 of 16 and the picture 15 of 16. Where it depends on contacts (the stack's push, the catapult's spring), the picture fixed 13 of 16 (Opus 5 of 8, GPT-6.1 Sol 8 of 8) and text only 1 of 16.
4. Text could tell the catapult was off but not by how much. All 8 text-only catapults worked out that the ball falls short and sent a fix; none landed in the bucket, and each then declared its fix worked. With the picture, the models saw the miss, fixed it, and saw it land.
5. The door was fixed by nobody: 0 of 16, in both arms. Its break leaves the outcome as the brief says (the door shuts and stays shut) and makes only the cause wrong: the door slams through its limit in 0.03 s instead of swinging. No model sent a fix. A failure that does not show in the outcome is invisible to the picture too.
6. Without a picture, every broken world left unfixed was claimed to work: 23 of 23. With the picture, 11 of 12, but 8 of those were doors; of the other 4, 3.
7. The picture's fixes came over rounds; text's did not. With the picture, 23 of 28 fixes came in round 1 and 5 in rounds 2 and 3. With text only, all 17 came in round 1: once a blind fix missed, no later round rescued it.
8. The new briefs were mostly easy. Dominoes worked as first written 16 of 16, in both arms. The pendulum worked as first written 10 of 16; of the rest, the picture fixed 3 of 4 and text only 1 of 2. In the end: Opus 3 of 4 in each arm, GPT-6.1 Sol 4 of 4 in each.
9. Two flaws in the new tests were found from the runs, fixed, and rescored from the saved files, with old verdicts kept. The dominoes test failed a first domino that starts tipped (3 worlds); the pendulum test measured a cup with an entry ramp from the ramp's end (3 worlds). Before any run, the picture's residue was fixed to cover a door's and a pendulum's whole swing.
10. Embarrassment tests, both passed. The picture fixed more broken worlds than text only (28 against 17 of 40), and with the picture each model fixed more than half of its 20 (Opus 12, GPT-6.1 Sol 16).
11. So a picture of the run is needed exactly where the failure lives in the dynamics. Text is enough for what can be calculated, and neither catches a wrong cause behind a right-looking outcome.
